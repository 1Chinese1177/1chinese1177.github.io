Wisdom Across the Ages — background playback

This short recording shows why the app uses a media-playback foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK): a narrated episode keeps playing after the listener leaves the app, with playback controls in the notification and on the lock screen.

  1. The listener opens an episode ("The Descent of Ishtar") and presses Play.
  2. The player screen shows the episode playing.
  3. The listener goes to the home screen. The episode keeps playing.
  4. The notification shade shows the media controls (play/pause, skip ±10 s, progress), and the listener pauses and resumes from there.
