Wisdom Across the Ages — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Wisdom Across the Ages ("the app") is published by DBO E-Commerce Services, LLC. It is an audio library of narrated ancient texts.

The short version

  • All 124 episodes, in both narrators' voices, are installed together with the app. Listening, reading along and the "about this text" notes work completely offline. Nothing streams and nothing is downloaded later.
  • There is no account and no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email address or any personal details.
  • Your listening position, streaks, quiz results and settings are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • The free tier shows ads from Google AdMob. The ad SDK is the only part of the app that uses the internet.
  • If you buy the ad-free upgrade, the purchase is handled by Google Play. The app receives only a confirmation that the upgrade is active; it never sees your payment details.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

On the free tier a banner appears on the library screen and a short ad plays between some episodes. When an ad is requested, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google and its partners: the device's advertising ID (which you can reset or delete in Android settings), approximate location derived from your IP address, device and app information (model, OS version, language, app package name), and diagnostic information about ad requests. This is used to serve, measure and improve ads and to prevent fraud, as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy. Where required by law, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads and you can change your choice from Settings.

Optional evening reminder

If you turn on the evening reminder, a notification is scheduled locally on your device. No push service is involved and nothing leaves the phone.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Data deletion

The app stores nothing about you on our servers, so there is nothing for us to delete. Uninstalling removes all locally stored data. Advertising data held by Google is governed by Google's policies and your Android advertising settings.
