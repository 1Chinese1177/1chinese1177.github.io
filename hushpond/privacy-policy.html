Hushpond — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Hushpond: Kids Sensory Games ("the app") is published by DBO E-Commerce Services, LLC ("we", "us"). The app is made for children aged about 5 to 10 and the grown-ups who look after them. This policy explains what the app does with information. It applies to the app on every device and to this page.

The short version

  • We do not collect any personal information from children or from anyone else. Nothing about your child ever leaves the device through the app.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts, no chat and no links to social media in the app.
  • The few things the app remembers (a profile with an optional nickname, comfort settings, favorites and optional play history) stay on your device, are excluded from cloud backups, and can be deleted at any time.
  • Subscriptions are bought by a grown-up through Google Play, inside the parent area, which is protected by a parental gate. We never receive payment details.

This information is kept in the app's private storage on the device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is excluded from Android cloud backups and device-to-device transfers. Uninstalling the app, or using Grown-ups › Privacy & data › Erase everything, removes all of it.

Information we collect

None. The app has no account system and no server of ours to send anything to. It contains no analytics, advertising, attribution or crash-reporting software. The app's own pages are blocked from making any internet connection; it plays fully offline.

Microphone (optional, off by default)

One activity, "Blowing Dandelion Seeds", lets a child blow at the phone to scatter the seeds. This works only if a grown-up turns it on in the parent area, which is when Android asks for the microphone permission. While the child uses that activity's microphone button, the app measures how loud the sound is, moment by moment, on the device. Sound is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. Touch and swipe always work instead, and the permission can be removed at any time in Android settings.

Spoken instructions

If spoken instructions are turned on, the app passes its own fixed instruction sentences (for example "Tap the lily pads") to the text-to-speech voice that is built into your device. No information about your child is included.

Purchases (Google Play)

Ten activities are free forever. A grown-up can subscribe to Hushpond Premium (monthly or yearly) to unlock all 100 activities. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play under the grown-up's Google account, only from the parent area behind the parental gate. Children never see prices or purchase prompts. We never receive card details, names or email addresses. The app asks Google Play whether the account has an active subscription and saves only the answer on the device. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled in Google Play. Purchases are covered by the Google Play Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

PDF summary

A grown-up can create a simple PDF summary of recent play from the parent area. It is created on the device and is only shared if the grown-up chooses to share it, using Android's share menu, with a person or app of their choice.

Permissions the app uses

  • Vibrate: optional gentle haptic feedback (can be turned off).
  • Microphone: only for the optional dandelion feature described above.
  • Internet, network state and Google Play Billing: used only by Google Play's purchase service for subscriptions. Gameplay never uses the internet.

The app does not use the camera, location, contacts, files, notifications or the advertising ID.

Google Play and Android

Google provides the Play Store, the purchase service and Android itself under its own privacy policy. If the device's owner allows it in Android settings, Google may share anonymous crash and performance statistics with app developers (for example, "the app stopped on an Android 13 phone"). These statistics do not identify your child and are not something the app sends itself.

Children's privacy

Hushpond is designed for children and follows the Google Play Families policies. We do not collect, use or disclose personal information from children, so no information about your child is held by us. Parents and caregivers can see and delete everything the app stores on the device at any time in Grown-ups › Privacy & data (clear history, delete optional data, or erase everything). The parent area is protected by a parental gate, and outside links such as Google Play's subscription page can only be opened from there.

Data deletion

Because we hold no data about you or your child, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. To remove everything the app stores, use Grown-ups › Privacy & data › Erase everything, clear the app's data in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Subscription records are kept by Google under your Google account.

Not a medical service

Hushpond is designed for children's entertainment and educational play. It is not a medical or therapy service and is not intended to diagnose or treat any health or developmental condition.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address and inside the app, with a new date. If the app ever starts collecting information, this policy will be updated before that happens.
