KJV Bible Trivia — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

KJV Bible Trivia ("the app") is published by DBO E-Commerce Services, LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with information when you use it.

The short version

  • The app works completely offline. All Bible text, chapter summaries and quiz questions are stored inside the app.
  • There is no account, no sign-in, and we never ask for your name, email address or any other personal details.
  • Your quiz progress, scores and settings are stored only on your device. They are deleted when you uninstall the app or use "Reset progress" in Settings.
  • The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. The ad SDK is the only part of the app that uses the internet.

Information the app stores on your device

Chapter completion, quiz scores, star ratings, recent rounds and the haptics setting are saved in a local database on your phone. This information never leaves your device and we cannot see it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the app free we show a banner ad on the Home and Results screens and an interstitial ad after every third quiz. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google and its advertising partners:

  • the device's advertising ID (which you can reset or delete in Android settings);
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • device and app information such as model, operating system version, language, and app package name;
  • diagnostic information about ad requests and interactions.

This information is used to serve, measure and improve ads, and to prevent fraud. How Google uses it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, and you can change your choice at any time from the app's Settings screen. You can also opt out of ad personalisation on your device under Android Settings → Google → Ads.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Data deletion

Because the app stores nothing about you on our servers, there is nothing for us to delete. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored progress. Advertising data held by Google is governed by Google's own policies and your Android advertising settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
