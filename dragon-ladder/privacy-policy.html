Dragon Ladder — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Dragon Ladder ("the game") is published by DBO E-Commerce Services, LLC. It is a single-player game that works entirely on your device.

The short version

  • No account. The game never asks you to sign in.
  • No data collection. The game does not collect, transmit or sell personal data. There is no analytics, no advertising SDK and no crash-reporting service.
  • Saves stay with you. Your expedition is stored locally on your device. Deleting the game deletes the save.
  • One optional purchase. The Full World unlock is processed by Google Play under Google's own privacy terms. The game receives only a purchase confirmation so it can open the rest of the Ladder; it never sees your payment details.
  • Permissions. Android: vibration (haptic feedback) only.
  • Children. The game contains no chat, no user-generated content and no external links beyond the Google Play purchase flow.

Data deletion

Nothing about you is stored on our servers, so there is nothing for us to delete. Uninstalling the game removes the local save. Purchase records are held by Google Play and governed by Google's policies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
