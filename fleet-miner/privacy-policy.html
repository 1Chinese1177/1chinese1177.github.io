Fleet Miner: Idle Space Tycoon — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Fleet Miner: Idle Space Tycoon ("the game") is published by DBO E-Commerce Services, LLC (game brand: DBO Studios). It is an idle space mining game.

The short version

  • There is no account and no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email address or any personal details.
  • Your progress (credits, sites, research, crew, items, gems and settings) is saved only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game.
  • The game never shows ads on its own. Ads play only when you choose to watch one for a reward (a production boost, a free supply crate, or doubled offline earnings). Those ads come from Google AdMob.
  • Nova Gems, the Starter Pack, the Captain's Pass and "No Ads + permanent x2" are sold through Google Play. The game never sees your payment details.
  • The ad SDK and Google Play's purchase service are the only parts of the game that use the internet.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

When you choose to watch a rewarded ad, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google and its partners: the device's advertising ID (which you can reset or delete in Android settings), approximate location derived from your IP address, device and app information (model, OS version, language, app package name), and diagnostic information about ad requests. This is used to serve, measure and improve ads and to prevent fraud, as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy. Where required by law (for example in the EU, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before ads can be personalised, and you can change your choice at any time from the Shop tab ("Ad privacy choices"). Players who own "No Ads" get every ad reward without watching an ad.

Purchases (Google Play)

Real-money items are one-time purchases made through Google Play. Payments are processed entirely by Google under the Google Play Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy; we never receive your card details, name or email address. To deliver what you bought, the game asks Google Play on your phone which Fleet Miner purchases your Google account has made and stores only the result (the items or perks granted, and a purchase reference so nothing is delivered twice) in the save file on your device. "No Ads + permanent x2" is restored automatically if you reinstall the game on the same Google account. Refunds are handled by Google Play. Supply Crate odds are shown in the game before you open a crate.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Data deletion

The game stores nothing about you on our servers, so there is nothing for us to delete. Uninstalling removes all locally stored data. Advertising data held by Google is governed by Google's policies and your Android advertising settings.
